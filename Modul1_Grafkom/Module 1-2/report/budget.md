## Lab 1 -- real resolution

Input: **no own photos were available -> fallback `make data`** (21 deterministic synthetic images, `data/manifest.csv`). The budget below assumes a typical 12 MP phone camera (4000x3000) and a 15-image capture set, the Lab 1 minimum.

## Budget at the real resolution

```
bytes / decoded image (RGB888) = 4000 x 3000 x 3 = 36,000,000 B = 36.00 MB (10^6) = 34.33 MiB (2^20)
working set, 15 images         = 540,000,000 B = 515.0 MiB (2^20)
flythrough 10 s @ 30 fps        = 10,800,000,000 B = 10.80 GB (10^9) = 10.06 GiB (2^30)
bandwidth                       = 1,080,000,000 B/s = 1080.0 MB/s = 8640.0 Mbit/s (10^6)
```

## Capstone texture budget (A1)

```
2048x2048 RGBA8:   16.0 MiB, with mips  21.33 MiB ->   384 fit in 8 GiB (all of it), ~ 192 in a realistic half
     compressed BC7 / ASTC 4x4 (8 bpp)  :   5.33 MiB -> ~  768 in half of 8 GiB
     compressed BC1 / ASTC 6x6 (~4 bpp) :   2.67 MiB -> ~ 1536 in half of 8 GiB
4096x4096 RGBA8:   64.0 MiB, with mips  85.33 MiB ->    96 fit in 8 GiB (all of it), ~  48 in a realistic half
     compressed BC7 / ASTC 4x4 (8 bpp)  :  21.33 MiB -> ~  192 in half of 8 GiB
     compressed BC1 / ASTC 6x6 (~4 bpp) :  10.67 MiB -> ~  384 in half of 8 GiB
200 textures @ 4K + mips uncompressed = 16.7 GiB  (> 2x the card)
```
